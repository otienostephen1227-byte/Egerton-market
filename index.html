🤝 How it works

1. A seller uploads a photo and item details.

2. A buyer searches the marketplace and contacts the seller.

3. They agree on a price, meet in a safe public place and complete the deal.

4. The seller marks the item as sold.

Future plan: introduce a transparent commission model after the marketplace is connected to a shared database and a reliable way to verify completed sales.
